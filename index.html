Our Story

Started with one cup and a lot of curiosity

Tima's Roast began as a small corner in F-7 where coffee was taken seriously. We were tired of burnt espresso and sugary syrups masking bad beans.

Five years later, the corner is still small. The curiosity has not gone anywhere.

  • Beans never older than 10 days
  • Filtered water for every brew
  • No artificial flavours
  • Recyclable takeaway cups
